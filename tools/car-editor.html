Colour sets

Each set is a paint plus matching zone colours (rims, interior …). The effects pick one set per car; a higher weight makes it more frequent. “Original” keeps the model's own colour. No sets = always original. Zones without their own colour in a set use the zone colour below.

Colour zones

Own areas with a fixed colour, e.g. interior black or trim chrome. Add a zone, press “Pick parts”, then click the parts on the car. A part belongs to one zone only; zones win over paint and rims. A colour set can give a zone another colour.

Parts

The paint colour goes to the automatically detected paint material. To colour single parts (body, rims, trim …) use a colour zone below.

If one click marks too much (e.g. the whole rim with the hub): press “Split a part”, click it – it falls apart into its pieces (parts without seams split at their edges: side wall, roof, single window panes), then pick as usual. Windows: untick “Click through glass” first. Picks on the whole part carry over to all pieces. If a part has no seams (one smooth surface, e.g. body panels joined from the mirror to the engine cover): press “Cut a part”, click the part, then click two points – it is cut straight along that line as seen from the camera (Esc cancels; cut pieces can be cut again). Badges whose motif is only painted in the texture can't be split; their frame is cut away automatically.

Wheels

If the wheels turn together with other parts (e.g. the bonnet) or a truck has more axles: pick the wheel parts yourself – tyre, rim, brake. They are grouped into wheels by position; only they turn. Two separate picks, a part may be in both: “Pick rolling parts” = what turns while driving (tyres, rims – not the brake calipers); “Pick steering parts” = what turns with the steering (front tyres and rims plus their brake calipers). A wheel steers as soon as one of its parts steers. Without steering parts the foremost axle steers. Colours: green = rolls and steers, orange = rolls only, blue = steers only.

“Add missing pieces”: some models split a tyre or rim into several pieces. If only some of them are picked, the rest stands still while the wheel turns and it looks wobbly – this adds the pieces of the same part that sit inside a picked wheel.

Ride height

Raise or lower the body at the front and rear axle (cm, + = higher). The wheels stay on the ground, the body moves – different values tilt it. Applies in the chat race and in Car Drive after saving.

Driver

A rubber duck sits in the driver's seat during races. Without your own seat it is placed automatically (left seat, behind the windscreen, sized to fit under the roof). “Place driver”: click the seat in the 3D view – the duck sits on that spot (keep “Click through glass” ticked to reach the seat behind the windows). Fine-tune with the sliders and number fields or drag the duck with Move/Rotate; “Auto place” goes back to the automatic seat.

While Move or Rotate is on: arrow keys move forward/back and left/right (Shift = 5×), Page Up/Page Down height, Q/E turn, Esc ends.

Walls (the duck is cut off beyond them)

Race stats

Real values for the chat car race. Until you change something they are an estimate (known car or car class), shown after each field. Cornering grip and braking matter most on the twisty tracks, top speed only a little; the race squeezes the differences, so a slow car keeps a small chance.

Steering lock = largest front-wheel angle, used when you drive the car yourself (Car Drive) and for the turning circle in tight hairpins of the chat race (Kart). Typical cars 35–40°, race cars about 30°, Formula 1 about 24°, buses and trucks up to 45°.

Drift = how willingly the car slides. High (powerful rear-wheel drive, muscle cars): the rear steps out gently and a slide can be held with throttle and countersteer, the handbrake turns it in more easily; in the chat race a bit faster through hairpins, with slightly more risk of going off. Low (front-wheel drive, race cars with downforce, trucks): grippy and safe, the rear holds more grip – catching a slide is up to the driver.

Record class = the class this car's laps count in for the class records per track (Car Drive, !records).

Engine sound = recorded engine type for Car Drive and the race overlay (close-ups, fly-bys). Play: start-up, two revs, a run through the gears, idle.

Sound

Engine sound of this car (Car Drive, race overlay). Until you change something here, the automatic sound plays (pieces cut by the sound builder). Mark the right parts of one recording yourself, set the engine values and try them in the gear simulator. The engine type is set under Race stats.

Electric engines have no recordings to mark and no gears.

Drag a marker line to move it. Keyboard: focus the waveform, Up/Down picks a marker, Left/Right moves it (Shift = 10×), + and − zoom. Grey = waveform, pink line = measured RPM.
