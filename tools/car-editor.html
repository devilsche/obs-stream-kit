Paints

The rain picks one paint per car; a higher weight makes it more frequent. “Original” keeps the model's own colour. No paints = always original.

Which parts get the colours

Press a button, then click on the car: each click adds or removes exactly that part. Paint parts show pink, rim parts cyan while picking. “Clear” empties the list so only what you click counts.

Wheels

If the wheels turn together with other parts (e.g. the bonnet) or a truck has more axles: pick the wheel parts yourself – tyre, rim, brake. They are grouped into wheels by position; only they turn and steer.
