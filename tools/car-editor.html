Colour sets

Each set is a paint plus matching zone colours (rims, interior …). The effects pick one set per car; a higher weight makes it more frequent. “Original” keeps the model's own colour. No sets = always original. Zones without their own colour in a set use the zone colour below.

Colour zones

Own areas with a fixed colour, e.g. interior black or trim chrome. Add a zone, press “Pick parts”, then click the parts on the car. A part belongs to one zone only; zones win over paint and rims. A colour set can give a zone another colour.

Which parts get the colours

Press a button, then click on the car: each click adds or removes exactly that part. Paint parts show pink while picking. “Clear paint” empties the list so only what you click counts. Rims are a colour zone (“Rims”) like the others.

If one click marks too much (e.g. the whole rim with the hub): press “Split a part”, click it – it falls apart into its pieces, then pick as usual. Picks on the whole part carry over to all pieces.

Wheels

If the wheels turn together with other parts (e.g. the bonnet) or a truck has more axles: pick the wheel parts yourself – tyre, rim, brake. They are grouped into wheels by position; only they turn. Steering wheels (green) go in “Pick front wheel parts”, all others (orange) in “Pick wheel parts” – e.g. a truck with two rear axles. Without front wheels the foremost axle steers.
