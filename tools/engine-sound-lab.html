Control

Keyboard: buttons with Enter/Space. Rev = throttle blip at standstill. Accelerate = full throttle from standstill to top speed through the gears (switches to Gearbox). Lift off = throttle closed at the current speed (overrun, crackle). Shift up = gear change now (RPM drop + short load cut). Hold throttle = full throttle through the gears up to top speed (top gear stays below the rev limiter). Hit limiter = full throttle in neutral, the engine bounces off the rev limiter until you press it again or another button. In Manual, RPM slider at the top with throttle above 50 % also hits the limiter.
