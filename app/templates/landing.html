build Tools (im Dashboard)

Session Report

Detailreport pro Session: Matches mit Squad-Tabelle, Mini-Map mit Bewegungs-Pfaden, Kills/Knocks pro Spieler, Hot-Drop-Detection, Achievements, Botteam-Counts. Pro Match ausklappbar mit Zeitraffer- Replay.

Match Replay

Full match replay with all 100 players, teams, Flugroute, Bluezone, Kill-/Knock-Markern, Schusslinien. 1×/2×/4×/ 8×/16× speed. Team focus with zoom. Filter across all stored Matches.

Landing Spots

Heatmap of landing spots across all matches — yours and your squad mates, filterable per map.

Achievement Browser

Full-screen browser through all Steam & PUBG achievements. Filter by game, sort by date/rarity/name. Click-to-replay triggers the popup widget for stream recaps.

settings Wie es funktioniert

  1. Log in with Twitch (OAuth) — an admin approves your account.
  2. In the settings, enter your PUBG-API-Key (kostenlos, developer.pubg.com) and your Steam-API-Key (steamcommunity.com/dev) hinterlegen.
  3. The server polls your PUBG and Steam data every 10 s in the background and fills your database tables.
  4. Auf /app/urls you get a token URL for every widget that you paste into OBS as a browser source.
  5. Tools liegen unter /app/tools (dashboard, not an OBS browser source).
